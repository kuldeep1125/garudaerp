     1→     1→# Business Management SaaS — Complete Product Requirements Specification
     2→     2→
     3→     3→## 1. Product Overview
     4→     4→
     5→     5→The system will be a **centralized business management SaaS/PWA for a group of business owners** to manage two separate businesses from one secure platform:
     6→     6→
     7→     7→1. **Manpower / Staffing Supply Business**
     8→     8→   - Employees are supplied/deployed to restaurant properties.
     9→     9→   - Properties pay the business for deployed employees/shifts.
    10→    10→   - Employees are paid according to their agreed rates.
    11→    11→   - The system tracks deployments, attendance/work, property billing, collections, employee earnings, advances, expenses, profitability, and monthly settlements.
    12→    12→
    13→    13→2. **Transport / Vehicle Rental Business**
    14→    14→   - Business-owned vehicles are rented to clients or used for trips.
    15→    15→   - Each vehicle has independent revenue, expenses, EMI, maintenance, profitability, and history.
    16→    16→   - This business must remain financially and operationally separate from the manpower business.
    17→    17→
    18→    18→The platform should function as a **single business-control center for all owners**, with extremely simple day-to-day data entry and highly automated calculations, reporting, statements, and notifications.
    19→    19→
    20→    20→The application must be designed **mobile-first**, with a smooth app-like experience through a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, while also working properly on desktop/laptop screens.
    21→    21→
    22→    22→---

    24→    24→# 2. Core Product Goals
    25→    25→
    26→    26→The system should be designed around the following principles:
    27→    27→
    28→    28→### 2.1 Minimum Data Entry
    29→    29→
    30→    30→Owners should not have to repeatedly enter information that the system already knows.
    31→    31→
    32→    32→For example:
    33→    33→
    34→    34→- Employee details are entered once.
    35→    35→- Property details are entered once.
    36→    36→- Standard rates are stored once.
    37→    37→- Vehicle information is entered once.
    38→    38→- Recurring expenses can be configured once.
    39→    39→- Monthly employee statements should be generated automatically from recorded work.
    40→    40→
    41→    41→### 2.2 Maximum Automation
    42→    42→
    43→    43→The system should automatically:
    44→    44→
    45→    45→- Calculate daily billing.
    46→    46→- Calculate employee earnings.
    47→    47→- Calculate business margins.
    48→    48→- Calculate monthly employee totals.
    49→    49→- Deduct advances.
    50→    50→- Generate monthly statements.
    51→    51→- Generate PDFs.
    52→    52→- Prepare WhatsApp messages.
    53→    53→- Calculate revenue and expenses.
    54→    54→- Calculate profit/loss.
    55→    55→- Produce daily/weekly/monthly reports.
    56→    56→- Identify unpaid property amounts.
    57→    57→- Calculate outstanding employee advances.
    58→    58→- Calculate vehicle profitability.
    59→    59→- Generate recurring financial records where configured.
    60→    60→
    61→    61→### 2.3 One Source of Truth
    62→    62→
    63→    63→All business records should exist in one centralized database.
    64→    64→
    65→    65→The same data should be reused across:
    66→    66→
    67→    67→- Dashboards
    68→    68→- Reports
    69→    69→- Employee statements
    70→    70→- Property statements
    71→    71→- Expense reports
    72→    72→- Profit/loss reports
    73→    73→- Owner reports
    74→    74→- Vehicle reports
    75→    75→- Monthly settlements
    76→    76→
    77→    77→There should not be separate manually maintained Excel-style datasets for different reports.
    78→    78→

   450→   450→
   451→   451→The application must automatically calculate daily financial totals.
